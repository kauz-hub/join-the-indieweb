13. Discover the independent web

Search engines, blog directories, webrings and personal recommendations can help you find pages that are often buried in mainstream search results.

Alternative search engines

Service What to explore
Marginalia Search Independent open-source search with an emphasis on non-commercial sites and exploratory discovery.
Wiby Classic and lightweight web pages. Try the surprise link.
Searchmysite.net Search across independently maintained personal websites.
SearXNG Open-source metasearch software; different from an independent crawler/index.
Mojeek Search with its own web index.
Kagi Small Web Discover posts from a curated collection of personal sites.

These are useful for exploration, not automatic fact-checking. An independent search result can still be inaccurate.

Directories, random sites and blog discovery

  • indieblog.page: discover a blog post at random.
  • IndieSeas: explore the independent web.
  • ooh.directory: browse blogs by topic.
  • Personal Sites: discover personal homepages.
  • Blogroll.org: manually curated blogs.
  • Neocities browse and Nekoweb: explore handmade websites.
  • Bear Blog discovery: explore posts and blogs.
  • JournalRing: a journal-oriented webring; review current membership instructions.

Old-web culture and archives

  • Protoweb: restored historical websites through a community-maintained proxy. Use a separate browser profile for historical browsing and don't enter sensitive data through legacy services.
  • The Old Net: explore historical web pages.
  • Wayback Machine: snapshots of websites over time.
  • WebTV Redialed: nostalgic recreation of the WebTV experience.
  • Agora Road's Macintosh Cafe: discussion forum with old-web culture; read community rules and judge content yourself.

Make browsing a habit

Pick a search term you enjoy, such as a hobby or favorite album. Open a few personal pages, follow their links and save interesting sites to your browser bookmarks or feed reader. Turn the best discoveries into a blogroll and explain why you like them. Find a webring whose theme suits your interests and participate if you want.
