hello, internet! ✧

Welcome to Join the IndieWeb, a small guide to making a little corner of the internet your own.

Maybe you want to start a blog, make a handmade homepage, or just find interesting places outside social media. You've come to the right place.

You don't need a perfect site or a fancy framework. Start with one page and see where it takes you.

New here? Start with the basics.
