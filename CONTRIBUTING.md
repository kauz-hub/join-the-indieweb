# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this guide useful for people who are new to the personal web.

## What belongs here?

- Clear explanations, first steps and realistic platform comparisons.
- Useful resources with a meaningful description.
- Corrections, broken-link fixes and accessibility improvements.
- Translations and examples of different approaches to making personal sites.

Keep the guide independent of any one host, paid service or design style. Website templates belong in separate repositories. Small markup examples that teach a standard are fine.

## Before submitting a change

1. Read the relevant chapter and check for duplicates.
2. Prefer primary documentation. Check claims about plan limits, supported features and setup steps against current official docs.
3. Add a link to your source and explain why the resource helps.
4. Use plain language and identify optional or advanced features.
5. Test relative links and check Markdown rendering.

Disclose any affiliation with a recommended service. Don't add tracking or affiliate URLs. Be respectful of people using different tools and levels of technical experience.
