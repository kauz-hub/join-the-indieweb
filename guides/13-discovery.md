# 13. Discover the independent web

[Home](../README.md) · [Next: personal web toolkit](14-personal-web-toolkit.md)

Search engines, blog directories, webrings and personal recommendations can help you find pages that are often buried in mainstream search results.

## Alternative search engines

| Service | What to explore |
|---|---|
| [Marginalia Search](https://marginalia-search.com/) | Independent open-source search with an emphasis on non-commercial sites and exploratory discovery. |
| [Wiby](https://wiby.me/) | Classic and lightweight web pages. Try the surprise link. |
| [Searchmysite.net](https://searchmysite.net/) | Search across independently maintained personal websites. |
| [SearXNG](https://docs.searxng.org/) | Open-source metasearch software; different from an independent crawler/index. |
| [Mojeek](https://www.mojeek.com/) | Search with its own web index. |
| [Kagi Small Web](https://kagi.com/smallweb/) | Discover posts from a curated collection of personal sites. |

These are useful for exploration, not automatic fact-checking. An independent search result can still be inaccurate.

## Directories, random sites and blog discovery

- [indieblog.page](https://indieblog.page/): discover a blog post at random.
- [IndieSeas](https://indieseas.net/): explore the independent web.
- [ooh.directory](https://ooh.directory/): browse blogs by topic.
- [Personal Sites](https://personalsit.es/): discover personal homepages.
- [Blogroll.org](https://blogroll.org/): manually curated blogs.
- [Neocities browse](https://neocities.org/browse) and [Nekoweb](https://nekoweb.org/): explore handmade websites.
- [Bear Blog discovery](https://bearblog.dev/discover/): explore posts and blogs.
- [JournalRing](https://nenrikido.neocities.org/webrings/journalring): a journal-oriented webring; review current membership instructions.

## Old-web culture and archives

- [Protoweb](https://protoweb.org/): restored historical websites through a community-maintained proxy. Use a separate browser profile for historical browsing and don't enter sensitive data through legacy services.
- [The Old Net](https://theoldnet.com/): explore historical web pages.
- [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/): snapshots of websites over time.
- [WebTV Redialed](https://webtv.zone/guide/index.html): nostalgic recreation of the WebTV experience.
- [Agora Road's Macintosh Cafe](https://forum.agoraroad.com/): discussion forum with old-web culture; read community rules and judge content yourself.

## Make browsing a habit

Pick a search term you enjoy, such as a hobby or favorite album. Open a few personal pages, follow their links and save interesting sites to your browser bookmarks or feed reader. Turn the best discoveries into a blogroll and explain why you like them. Find a webring whose theme suits your interests and participate if you want.
